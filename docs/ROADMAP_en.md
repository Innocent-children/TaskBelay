# Further product assessment

[中文](ROADMAP.md) | [English](ROADMAP_en.md)

The current engineering upgrade is accepted as a complete contract under [product](PRODUCT_en.md). This page does not divide its delivery into an MVP or promises of future capabilities.

Future changes need real task evidence of necessity and assessment of equivalent acceptance quality, necessary change magnitude, full verification cost, recovery correctness and user decisions. Platform/real Host support, long-term benefits and dependency coverage require separate validation; source builds and model scores cannot establish them. See [verification](PROJECT-STATUS_en.md) and [contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
